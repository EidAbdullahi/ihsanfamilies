Organization overview

Somali-led NGO for Health Systems Strengthening

Headquartered in Kismayo, Somalia, Ihsan Care integrates digital health, MNCH, capacity building, and community outreach to bridge systemic gaps. Our focus includes biometric registration, electronic health records, and donor transparency dashboards — delivering measurable impact in fragile settings.

Vision: Resilient, empowered communities & equitable healthcare
Mission: Digital solutions + capacity building + accountable systems

Our Programs & Services

Covering 6 core areas from digital systems to community resilience

1. Digital Health & Healthcare Systems

  • Digital beneficiary registration & biometric verification
  • Cashless healthcare access solutions
  • Electronic health records (EHR) & tracking systems
  • Real-time data dashboards for donors/partners
  • Health facility digitalization & HMIS support

2. Capacity Building & Institutional Strengthening

  • Training for midwives, nurses, CHWs
  • Health Information Systems (DHIS2) training
  • M&E systems development & data management
  • Project planning, NGO & government capacity strengthening

3. Health Education & Community Learning

  • Community health education (MNCH, nutrition, hygiene)
  • School-based health awareness & adolescent reproductive health
  • Adult literacy linked to health awareness
  • Digital literacy for HCWs & Training of Trainers (ToT)

4. Outreach & Behavior Change

  • Community mobilization, health campaigns
  • Behavior Change Communication (BCC) strategies
  • Nutrition & hygiene promotion, outreach for underserved populations

5. Research, Data & Innovation

Baseline surveys, needs assessments, health data analytics, operational research in digital health, mHealth pilots, biometric system pilots.

6. Integrated Development Support

WASH integration, nutrition support, primary healthcare infrastructure, emergency & humanitarian response.
